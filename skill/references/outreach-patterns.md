# Outreach patterns

## Draft formula

1. Opener tied to one observed clue
2. Short statement of likely pain
3. Why the offer is relevant
4. Simple CTA

## Good opener examples

- "Saw that your team works in regional logistics and handles time-sensitive operations."
- "Your site suggests a strong focus on construction delivery and project coordination."
- "It looks like you serve business clients in a regulated or document-heavy workflow."

## Avoid

- fake personalization
- invented names or job titles
- more than one CTA
- vague hype
- long founder-story intros

## Follow-up angle

If no reply, follow up with one of:
- practical example
- short audit offer
- quick checklist
- one relevant risk/update
